# Redesain placement Bahasa Swedia

Ilustrasi dibuat dengan built-in imagegen (bukan CLI), lalu dikompres menjadi WebP 1200px (sekitar 63 KB).

Aset: `public/illustrations/placement/swedish-study-v1.webp`.

Prompt final:
> Use case: illustration-story. Create a polished website hero illustration, landscape 3:2. Cute minimalist flat vector style with clean rounded shapes and restrained detail, like a friendly language learning app. A young adult woman with dark navy hair and teal hoodie, smiling with cheek resting in her hand while studying at a silver laptop on a cream desk. Small Swedish blue and yellow flag on a white mug, two blue and yellow books, small leafy plant. Behind her a pale mint arched window with a simplified Stockholm waterfront skyline. Teal pendant lamp. Warm peach cheeks, tiny dot eyes. Palette teal #1A9E9E, navy, pale mint, cream, Swedish blue and yellow. Very light near-white mint background, generous breathing space, entire scene contained with no cropped character. Minimal shadows, crisp vector-like solid fills, no painterly texture, no 3D. No text, no lettering, no watermark. This is only the illustration asset, no UI or buttons.

Desain berlaku khusus `/silabus/swedish/coba`. Tombol tes gratis memakai popup biodata/tips yang sudah ada. Tombol speaking memakai komponen checkout yang sudah ada dan harga dari pricelist resmi. Layout satu kolom di mobile, dua kolom di desktop; ilustrasi menggunakan next/image dengan prioritas untuk hero.
